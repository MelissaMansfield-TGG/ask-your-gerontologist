Episode 2, Part 1
Plant the Seed.
1
Start with what is already working.
Ask how her days feel and what she looks forward to.
2
Learn what she wants to keep doing.
Driving. Cooking. Living in her own home. That is what you are protecting, not taking away.
3
Name the worry. Ask permission first.
"Could we think together about what might make this easier for you?"
If she says not now
"We can leave it here for today."
And you mean it.
When the moment is right
Tie one small step to something she already loves.
Then give her room to decide.
