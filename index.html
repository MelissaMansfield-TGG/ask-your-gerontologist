What it means to plant the seed

An honest offer, then you step back. She makes the decision.

Three steps

  1. Start with what is already working. Ask how her days feel and what she looks forward to.
  2. Find out what she wants to keep doing on her own: driving, cooking, living in her own home.
  3. Name the worry gently and ask permission. "Could we think together about what might make this easier for you?"

If she says not now: "We can leave it here for today."

The question to sit with

Do you name the worry, and risk her telling you not yet?

Or do you stay quiet, and risk never finding out what she would have chosen?
